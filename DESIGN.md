# Lockstep

Lockstep is a compact desktop control for starting and stopping the existing Valorant agent-lock script. Its dark palette follows the game's pre-match setting and keeps long-running status readable at a glance. Warm amber marks the one primary action; green and red only communicate running and stopped states. The log is the main evidence of what the script did, so it receives more space than decoration or summary cards.

Identity: focused pre-match utility.
Personality: quiet, direct, deliberate.
Palette: charcoal surfaces, warm off-white text, one muted amber accent.
Typography: system sans for readable labels and a restrained monospace log for process output.
Mood: a dim control room, without glow, gradients, or animated ornament.
Dials: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
